## User

does godot 4.7.2 support 4x and higher Supersample antialiasing (SSAA)?

## Assistant

I will check Godot's documentation and rendering settings for SSAA support.

## Assistant

Godot 4.7.2 supports built-in 4x SSAA, but its source code caps the scale at 2.0 per axis. Higher SSAA levels require a custom rendering and downsampling setup.

## Assistant

**4x SSAA: yes. Higher than 4x: not through the built-in SSAA setting.**

To enable 4x SSAA, enable Advanced Settings in Project Settings, then set:

- **Rendering > Scaling 3D > Mode:** `Bilinear`
- **Rendering > Scaling 3D > Scale:** `2.0`

The scale applies to both dimensions, so `2.0` renders four times as many pixels. For example, 1920x1080 output is rendered internally at 3840x2160, then downsampled. [Godot 4.7 SSAA documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/3d/3d_antialiasing.html#supersample-antialiasing-ssaa)

Godot 4.7.2 explicitly clamps the scale to a maximum of `2.0`, including when set through code. Consequently, entering a larger value cannot enable 8x or 16x SSAA. [Godot 4.7.2 source](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/scene/main/viewport.cpp)

Going higher would require a custom approach, such as rendering into a larger `SubViewport` and applying a downsampling filter that averages the additional samples.
