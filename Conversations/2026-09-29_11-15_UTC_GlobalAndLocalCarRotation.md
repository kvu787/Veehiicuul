## Assistant

**Yes: they measure rotation relative to different coordinate systems.**

- `Rotation.Y`: the node’s Y rotation **relative to its parent**.
- `GlobalRotation.Y`: the node’s Y rotation **relative to the world**, including inherited transforms.

Both are in **radians**. The leading `-` simply negates whichever value you read. [Godot documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_node3d.html#class-node3d-property-global-rotation)

For example, with only Y rotations, if the parent is rotated 30° and the car is rotated another 20° relative to it:

```csharp
-car.Rotation.Y        // -20° expressed in radians
-car.GlobalRotation.Y  // -50° expressed in radians
```

**In your current Ribeye scene, they should give the same yaw** because the placeholder’s ancestors have identity transforms.

I would keep `GlobalRotation.Y` in [CarStateManager.cs](C:/Users/k/Repository/Veehiicuul/Veehiicuul_Godot_CSharp/Veehiicuul/Source/GameDataAndLogic/Car/CarStateManager.cs:41): the manager stores world-space position and applies world-space rotation.
